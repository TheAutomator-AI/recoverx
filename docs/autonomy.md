# RecoverX — Autonomy Gating & Confidence Calibration

## Overview

RecoverX replaces opaque all-or-nothing AI execution with **Confidence-Gated Autonomy**. The system evaluates structured diagnostic evidence to compute a calibrated confidence score, which partitions decisions into three distinct operating modes:

1. **AUTONOMOUS** ($\ge 0.85$ composite confidence + Policy APPROVE)
2. **ASSISTED** ($0.60 - 0.849$ composite confidence or Policy ESCALATE)
3. **ESCALATED** ($< 0.60$ composite confidence or Contradictory Telemetry)

---

## Confidence Calibration Formula

Blindly trusting raw model confidence (LLM self-assessment) causes severe overconfidence on ambiguous cases. RecoverX enforces an explicit multi-factor scoring engine:

$$\text{Composite Confidence} = \sum_{i=1}^{5} (s_i \times w_i)$$

Where:
* $s_1$: **Reason Clarity Score** (Weight $w_1 = 35\%$)
* $s_2$: **Historical Pattern Score** (Weight $w_2 = 25\%$)
* $s_3$: **Context Completeness Score** (Weight $w_3 = 20\%$)
* $s_4$: **Action History Score** (Weight $w_4 = 10\%$)
* $s_5$: **Model Assessment Score** (Weight $w_5 = 10\%$)

$$\sum w_i = 0.35 + 0.25 + 0.20 + 0.10 + 0.10 = 1.00$$

### Factor Breakdown

#### 1. Reason Clarity ($35\%$)
* Analyzes whether the failure step is distinct (e.g. `TIMEOUT`, `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, `CARD_EXPIRED` vs `UNKNOWN`).
* Validates presence of standard bank error codes (e.g. `NPCI_ERR_91`, `NPCI_ERR_51`).
* **Severe Contradiction Penalty**: If gateway and bank callbacks conflict, clarity score drops to $0.10$ and composite confidence is capped at $\le 0.48$.

#### 2. Historical Pattern ($25\%$)
* Customer historical payment success rate baseline ($\approx 0.88 - 0.92$).
* Customer segment reliability (VIP, Enterprise, D2C).
* Broken promise penalty (deducts $0.15$ per broken promise).

#### 3. Context Completeness ($20\%$)
* Evaluates availability of customer metadata (name, language, script, payment method, LTV).

#### 4. Action History ($10\%$)
* Evaluates attempt progression:
  * Attempt 1: $0.90$
  * Attempt 2: $0.65$
  * Attempt 3: $0.35$
  * Attempt 4+: $0.10$

#### 5. Model Assessment ($10\%$)
* Raw diagnostic confidence generated by the AI provider bounded in $[0.0, 1.0]$.

## Autonomy Modes

### 1. AUTONOMOUS Mode
* **Criteria**: Composite Confidence $\ge 0.85$ AND all Policy Engine rules evaluated to `APPROVE`.
* **Action**: Automated simulated retry dispatch or scheduled retry.
* **Verification**: Transaction verified via bank gateway callback before marking recovered.

### 2. ASSISTED Mode
* **Criteria**: Composite Confidence in $[0.60, 0.849]$ OR transaction requires human oversight (e.g. high value).
* **Action**: Enqueued in the **Human Review Queue**.
* **Reviewer Powers**:
  * **APPROVE**: Authorize the AI recommendation.
  * **MODIFY**: Adjust action, retry delay, or communication channel.
  * **REJECT**: Terminate recovery.
* **Metrics Tracked**: Overturn rate, modification rate, review duration.

### 3. ESCALATED Mode
* **Criteria**: Composite Confidence $< 0.60$ OR contradictory telemetry detected.
* **Action**: Financial execution halted immediately. Sent to Senior Operations review.
* **Goal**: Prevent double debits, merchant penalties, and customer churn.
